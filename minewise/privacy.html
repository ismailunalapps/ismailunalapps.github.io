MineWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

MineWise is a puzzle game made by İsmail Ünal, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the MineWise apps for Android and iOS.

In short: MineWise has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. To keep the game free, it shows ads through Google AdMob, and it uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played. Where the law requires it, both only run with your consent.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only. It is not sent to me:

  • Your settings (difficulty, game mode, theme, flag mode, whether you have seen the tutorial)
  • Your statistics (games played, wins, best times, win streaks)
  • Your power-ups and purchases (for example, Safe Reveals and unlocked themes)
  • When you first opened the game and when you last tried a theme, so that free theme trials and offers are limited to once a day

Deleting the app deletes this information.

2. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

I use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is used, for example which difficulty levels are played, how long games last, and whether features such as the shop or theme trials work well. This helps me fix problems and improve the game.

Firebase collects:

  • Gameplay events, such as a game starting or ending (won, lost or abandoned, and how long it took), changing game mode, opening the shop, starting a theme trial, and purchase attempts and results (product only, never payment details)
  • An app instance ID, which is a random identifier for this installation of the game
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address)

Firebase does not receive your name, email or contacts. Google processes this data as described in Firebase's privacy information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

MineWise shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded videos. These are optional. You choose to watch one in the shop to earn a Safe Reveal.
  • Full-screen ads between games. These never appear on your first day of play, and at most every few games. Buying "Remove Ads" or "Premium" turns them off.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID. On Android this is the Advertising ID. On iOS this is the IDFA, which is only used if you allow tracking when asked.
  • IP address, device and app information
  • How you interact with ads

Ads may be personalised based on your interests only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you will see non-personalised ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. When you first open the game you are asked for consent through Google's consent form. Analytics and personalised ads only run if you agree. You can change your choice at any time from Statistics → Privacy in the game.
  • iOS. The game asks for permission before using your IDFA for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads, depending on the device.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. I never receive your payment card details. I only learn which item was bought, so it can be unlocked in the game.

6. Why this information is used (legal basis)

Where the EU or UK GDPR or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) apply:

  • Analytics and personalised ads: your consent.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the game working: legitimate interests in offering a free game funded by advertising.
  • Purchases: to deliver what you bought.

7. Sharing and international transfers

I do not sell your personal information. The information described above is shared only with the service providers named in this policy (Google for Firebase and AdMob, and Google Play or Apple for purchases). These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long data is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Analytics data linked to the app instance ID is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Children

MineWise is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and data has been collected, contact me and I will help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the game has no accounts, I cannot identify you from your email alone. To make a request, write to me and I will explain how to find the identifiers needed to locate your data. You can also withdraw consent in the game, as described in section 4, and delete your on-device data by deleting the app.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
